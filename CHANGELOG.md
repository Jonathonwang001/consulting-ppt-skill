## v2.0.0 — 2026-11-18

### 新增 / Added

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|

| 升级中英文README、版本说明与原创内容MIT许可证 | Updated bilingual README, release notes and MIT license for original content |

### 修订 / Changed

保留原版咨询框架、三点论、11种页面和11种模块，将“按页型套版”升级为“按客户决策和内容关系设计”。重新整理五份既有参考文件，新增按需加载入口；客户品牌优先，机构风格是启发式预设而非官方规范。

Preserves the original consulting frameworks, three persuasion goals, 11 slide types and 11 modules while replacing template-first design with client-decision and relationship-driven planning. References are updated with progressive loading; client branding overrides non-official firm-inspired presets.

### 纠正 / Fixed

取消“每页必须图标/每步骤强配图标”、每点15字/标题最少15字/内容占比60%、强制衬线、每个论点恰好三个子点与每图必加洞察框等绝对化规则。明确事实、初步假设、示意与真实身份边界；区分百分比和百分点、留存与阶段转化；不再默认以不可编辑图像作为咨询报告的最佳输出。

Removes mandatory per-slide icons and rigid word-count, serif-font, three-subpoint and callout rules. Clarifies factual versus hypothetical content, illustrative avatars, percentages versus percentage points, retention versus stage conversion, and editable authoring preferences.

### 保留 / Preserved

麦肯锡深蓝、罗兰贝格酒红、德勤黑/炭灰/蓝灰为主且绿色只点缀、BCG深绿；金字塔原理、Action Title、MECE及核心咨询工具。页数依当前任务与工具约束，不把教材中的历史经验当统一硬门槛。

Deep navy, wine red, charcoal/blue-grey with limited Deloitte green accents, and deep green remain available presets. Pyramid thinking, action titles, MECE and consulting tools remain core. Deck length follows the current task and host constraints rather than universalizing historical training norms.

详见 / Details: [v2.0.0升级说明 / Release notes](releases/v2.0.0.md)。
